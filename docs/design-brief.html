Design Brief — v1.0 — Approved Direction

Off-white paper, black ink, and nothing wasted.

A Swiss-editorial system for the AAIR Labs Centralized Web Portal. Generous whitespace, a strict grid, serif display type against a neutral sans — with color reserved almost entirely for state: green means go, orange means attention.

Design Principles

01
01

Hierarchy through type, not color

Size, weight and serif/sans contrast do the work. If a screen needs three colors to feel organized, the structure is wrong.

02

The grid is visible

Hairline rules and column alignment echo a research paper's margins. Elements snap to an 8px baseline.

03

Color is a signal

Green = approved / primary action. Orange = needs attention. Everything else is ink on paper. No decorative color.

04

Restrained motion

120–150ms state transitions only. Cards lift 2px on hover; nothing animates on scroll. Respect reduced-motion.

Usage Rules

06

Do

  • Use hairline 1px rules and 2px ink rules to separate sections, like a printed journal.
  • Set serif display type at large sizes with tight leading; let it carry the personality.
  • Pair every color-coded status with a text label.
  • Keep 8px spacing rhythm and a 62ch max measure for body text.
  • Reserve green for the single most important action on the screen.
  • Ensure WCAG 2.1 AA contrast — all ink/paper pairings here pass at 4.5:1 or better.

Don't

  • Don't fill hero sections or sidebars with green or orange.
  • Don't use drop shadows on cards — a border and 2px lift are enough.
  • Don't introduce a third typeface or round corners past 4px.
  • Don't use color as the only indicator of pipeline state.
  • Don't use official UTD trademarks or logos without university sponsorship.
  • Don't animate on scroll; motion is limited to hover and focus states.
